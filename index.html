Nigeria

Nigeria, officially the Federal Republic of Nigeria, is a West African nation on the Gulf of Guinea and is the most populous in Africa, often called the "Giant of Africa". It is a diverse, multinational state with over 250 ethnic groups including the dominant Hausa, Igbo,and Yoruba, more than 500 languages and a mix of Christian and Muslim populations.
